  • Check for last minute surprises:

    If new fees, mandatory add ons, or changed prices appear only at the final checkout step, treat this as a signal of potential price related dark patterns and reconsider proceeding.

  • Inspect defaults and checkboxes carefully:

    Before confirming, scan for pre ticked boxes related to subscriptions, marketing communications, or data sharing, and deliberately set them to your true preferences.

  • Test the ease of saying “no”:

    Ethical interfaces make it as simple to decline as to accept; if “reject”, “opt out”, or “cancel” options are visually weak, hidden, or linguistically shaming, you may be encountering obstruction or confirmshaming.

  • Treat extreme urgency and popularity claims with scepticism:

    Always question countdown timers, “only X left” messages, and “Y others are viewing now”, especially when they appear on nearly every product or reset repeatedly.

  • Look beyond ratings and badges:

    When reviews are uniformly positive, critical feedback is hard to locate, or trust badges lack transparent verification, assume that the social proof may be curated rather than neutral.
